Upstream Price Pressures and Consumer Inflation: Testing Supply Chain Price Transmission in the U.S.

Python · NumPy · SciPy

Tested whether upstream price pressures — at the producer and import stage — systematically lead U.S. consumer prices, linking the bullwhip effect to macro-empirical inflation evidence. Analyzed CPI, PPI (Final Demand), and Import Price Index data (BLS, Jan 2015–Jul 2026) using cross-correlation and pairwise Granger-causality F-tests.

p = 0.021 import prices → CPI, 1-month lag · one-way, CPI never Granger-causes import prices

Finding — import prices Granger-cause CPI without reverse causality; producer and consumer prices show strong bidirectional causality.
