About

I'm Rabin, a data scientist working at the intersection of data science, business analytics, and quantitative decision-making. The question underneath most of what I do: how do shocks and signals move through a supply chain, and how can interpretable machine learning make that movement legible to the people who have to act on it.

My recent work traces that question from macro price transmission between producers and consumers down to firm-level demand and risk prediction — most recently a working paper testing whether upstream price pressures Granger-cause U.S. consumer inflation. My long-term goal is doctoral research developing explainable, data-driven methods for supply chain and business decision-making.

I currently work as a Junior Social Media Data Analyst at Kaebox, analyzing engagement data and translating findings into decisions, following completion of my M.S. in Data Science at Carolina University.
